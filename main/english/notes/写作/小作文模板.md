Dear John,
	I am very glad to hear that you are interested in the play based on a classic Chinese novel. I am writing this letter in order to provide further details about the play.
	The details can be listed as follows. The short play is adapted from Journey to the West, one of China's Four Great Classical Novels. Rehearsals will be scheduled on weekends at the student activity center, starting next month.

                                                                                                  Yours sincerely,
                                                                                                           Li Ming

## LLM 出题练习

### 四大发明

**Directions:**
 You are organizing a cultural exchange event for international students at your university. The organizing committee asks you to write a short **notice** introducing an upcoming exhibition on **the Four Great Inventions of ancient China**.

Write a **notice** to:

1. briefly introduce the theme of the exhibition,
2. highlight its main features, and                     
3. invite students to participate.

You should write **about 100 words** on the **ANSWER SHEET**.
 Do **not** use your own name. Use “The Students’ Association” instead.
 Do **not** write your address.

```
Notice
	It's a pleasure to inform everyone that an upcoming exhibition on the Four Great Inventions of ancient China will be held in our university. This notice is intended to introduce some details of this content.
	This event is held under the theme of "Tradition and Future". The event will commence with an opening ceremony, followed by a series of exhibitions about history, technology details from ancient Chinese books, and so on. It will conclude with a discussion session about future development and application of it, where participants can exchange ideas. The exhibition will be held in student activity center at 9:00 a.m. on Sunday, December 20th. 
	We sincerely look forward to your active participation and believe this exhibition will bring you an unforgettable experience! Please scan the QR code below to sign up!
The Students' Association,
December 20th
```
