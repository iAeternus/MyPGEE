Specifically, it increased gradually from 8.8% in 2012 to 11.58% in 2016, and then witnessed a remarkable rise to 25.40% in 2021. It is apparent that the health literacy level has been developing rapidly in recent years.

Three key reasons, in my view, can account for this trend. Firstly, the growing public awareness of health has encouraged individuals to proactively seek and absorb health-related knowledge. Secondly, the continuous improvement of medical infrastructure, such as the expansion of community health centers, has provided broader and more accessible platforms for public education. Lastly, the overall improvement in people’s health has inspired a stronger desire to maintain and enhance this positive momentum.

By no means should we cease our pursuit of health literacy growth. It is imperative for the authorities to allocate more funds to promote it, and individuals should spare no effort to exercise regularly. In this way, we can look forward to a healthier and brighter future.

## 意识练习

### 1

主题：就业价值观变化
主旨：如今越来越多的年轻人倾向于更稳定的工作
论点
1. 年轻人不愿意像父母那样辛苦 repeat the lifestyle of their parents / balanced and predictable
2. 就业选择不断丰富，稳定岗位的吸引力不断突出 compare different types of work / reliable and attractive
3. 社会压力变大，追求稳定 living costs / competition / steady and less stressful

### 2

主题: 体育强则国强
主旨: 青年越来越重视体育锻炼
论点

1. 客观：学校基础设施的发展 the infrastructure development of the university
2. 主观：青年的健康意识不断提高  young people / stronger health awareness
3. 上升：国家体育强国战略的不断落实 implementation / China has been promoting national strategies to build a strong sports nation

## 注意事项

1. 归因分析很重要，第二段不能只夸好处、只写意义
1. 总结社会现象，归因分析是英语二作文的常用技巧
1. 归因分析可以从客观原因、主观原因、上升政策来构思
