```
To increase the likelihood of success, one should set realistic goals and work persistently towards them. In the comtempoaray world, success has become increasingly significant. In modern society, success has gained critical significance, making it imperative for youngsters to set realistic goals and work persistently. Reasons and concrete evidence to support my view point are as follows.
First of all, it's obvious that realistic goals provide clear direction and motivation. Based on analysis of big data, most successful individuals admitted that they've spent a big part of their time planning and refining their objectives. Moreover, no one can deny that persistently is the key to overcoming challenges. Where there is a persistence, there is a success. Last but not least, I firmly believe that persistence fosters resilience and long-term success. The more effort you put into your goals, the more rewarding the results will be.
In conclusion, setting realistic goals and persisting througn challenges are indispensable for success. If we spare no efforts to stay focused and determined, the future of our endeavors will be both hopeful and rosy.
```

* 三个段落
  * 第一段 两句话 引出背景（点题） + 过渡
  * 第二段 六句话 三个论点 + 三个论据
  * 第三段 两句话 重申观点 + 提出希望 
* 第一段
  * In the contemporary world, ____ have/has become increasingly significant. It's of great necessity for ____ to ____.
    * 当今世界，...（主题词）已经变得越来越重要。对于某类人来说，做某些事非常的有必要。
    * 替换：**In modern society, __ __ has gained critical significance, making it imperative for __ __ to __ __.**
    * 在现代社会中，...（主题词）已经变得至关重要，因此某类人必须做某事。
  * Reasons and concrete evidence to support my view point are as follows.
    * 支持我观点的理由和具体证据如下。
* 第二段
  * 论点一 First of all, it is obvious that ... 
    * 首先，显而易见的是...
  * 论据一 Based on analysis of big data, most successful individuals admitted that they **'ve spent** two-thirds of their time ____.
  * 论点二 Moreover, no one can deny that ____.
    * 此外，无人可以否认...
  * 论据二 Where there is/are ____, there is/are a ____. (Where there is a will, there is a way 有志者事竟成)
    * 有...，就有...。
  * 论点三 Last but not least, I firmly believe that ____.
    * 最后我坚定地认为...
  * 论据三 The + 比较级 + 动词, the + 比较级 + 动词.
* 第三段
  * In conclusion, ____. If we spare no efforts to ____, the future of ____ will be both hopeful and rosy.
    * 总之，...。如果我们不遗余力地...，那么...的未来将会充满希望和美好。
